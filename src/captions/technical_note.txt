The image shows a colorized sub-aperture SAR image captured by Capella Space on 11 January 2025. SAR images are acquired over several seconds as the satellite looks at the area of interest from different perspectives. Objects that reflect differently from different angles appear colorful in SAR images. The destruction times are estimated from eight SAR images (7, Capella Space; 1 Umbra) and one optical satellite image from Vantor. The images are coregistered, and each building is segmented using building footprints from OpenStreetMap. Official inspection data (CAL FIRE damage inspection data) are used to train a classifier to classify a building as intact or destroyed. The classifier predicts the probability of destruction for individual buildings in each satellite image. A change-point detection model is then fitted to these probabilities to determine the most likely time of destruction. Detections are corrected using low-resolution fire data from a weather satellite (GOES-18) and infrared satellite data (VIIRS).
