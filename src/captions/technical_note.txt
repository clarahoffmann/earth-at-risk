The top panel shows a colorized sub-aperture SAR image formed using Capella Space data from 11 January 2025. SAR images are acquired over several seconds during which the satellite observes an area from different perspectives. Objects whose reflection varies with the look angle appear colorful in this image. The destruction times are estimated from eight SAR images (7 Capella Space; 1 Umbra) and one optical satellite image from Vantor. The images are coregistered, and buildings are segmented using OpenStreetMap footprints. A classifier trained on CAL FIRE damage inspection data predicts the probability of destruction for individual buildings in each image. A change-point detection model is then fitted to these probabilities to determine the most likely time of destruction. Detections are refined using infrared satellite data (GOES-18, VIIRS). The map shows 7,021 destroyed buildings in the analysis area. A further 265 buildings were not detected in the SAR images.
