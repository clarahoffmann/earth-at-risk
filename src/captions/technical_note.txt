The top panel shows a colorized sub-aperture SAR image captured by Capella Space on 11 January 2025. SAR images are acquired over several seconds during which the satellite observes an area from different perspectives. Objects whose reflection varies with the look angle appear colorful in these SAR images. The destruction times are estimated from eight SAR images (7 Capella Space; 1 Umbra) and one optical satellite image from Vantor. The images are coregistered, and each building is segmented using building footprints from OpenStreetMap. Official inspection data (CAL FIRE damage inspection data) are used to train a classifier, which predicts the probability of destruction for individual buildings in each image. A change-point detection model is then fitted to these probabilities to determine the most likely time of destruction. Detections are corrected using low-resolution fire data from two infrared satellite data (GOES-18, VIIRS).
