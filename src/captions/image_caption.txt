This Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) satellite image reveals the destruction of Altadena, Los Angeles County, US, during the 2025 Eaton fire. Each building is colored by its estimated destruction time computed from a time series of SAR and one optical satellite image. Low precipitation and dry desert winds increase wildfire risk in Los Angeles. The fire was ignited by a transmission tower connected to the power lines visible at the top of the image, which appear in bright rainbow colors. Power grids near vegetation can trigger devastating wildfires, yet effective countermeasures to prevent such fires remain insufficient.
