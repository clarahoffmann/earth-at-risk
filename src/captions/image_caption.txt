This Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) satellite image reveals the destruction of Altadena, Los Angeles County, US, during the 2025 Eaton fire. Each destroyed building is colored by its destruction time estimated from SAR and optical satellite imagery. The fire was ignited by a transmission tower (circle) connected to power lines (bright rainbow colors) and spread by strong, dry winds. The Eaton fire ranks among the most destructive fires caused by utility infrastructure. Power grids near vegetation, combined with low precipitation, create a hazardous fire risk in California and occur worldwide, as shown by the red areas on the bottom map.
